Rules I would use

  • Protect your $/online hour and $/total mile; don't chase trip count.
  • Count deadhead miles when judging airport and long-distance trips.
  • Use UberX/Comfort first; use delivery as a fallback when passenger demand is weak.
  • Track your real numbers for 4–6 weeks before changing vehicle or schedule decisions.
  • Keep a tax reserve and a vehicle-replacement reserve.
